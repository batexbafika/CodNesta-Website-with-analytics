Privacy Policy

1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to CodNesta Group. We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding the personal data you share with us across our websites, application platforms, developer portals, enterprise services, and training programs.

This Privacy Policy explains how CodNesta Group collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your information when you interact with our services, submit project inquiries, apply for academy cohorts, or use our digital platforms.

By accessing our website or engaging with CodNesta Group services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to deliver tech solutions, manage developer networks, and process business inquiries effectively.

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

You may provide personal information through direct submissions across our digital forms:

  • Personal & Contact Details: Full name, business email address, phone/WhatsApp number, physical address, and LinkedIn profile.
  • Project & Business Enquiries: Technical specifications submitted via forms regarding enterprise software requirements, strategic partnerships, or service requests.
  • Academy & Talent Applications: Educational background, programming skill sets, GitHub profiles, portfolio links, and resume/CV submissions.
  • Communication Records: Inquiries sent via direct messages, contact forms, or direct email correspondence.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When interacting with our online portals, technical parameters are automatically captured:

  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device specifications, operating system, and geographic region.
  • Platform Analytics: Pages viewed, duration of visits, referral URLs, interaction patterns, and performance logs collected via cookies and analytics tools.

3. How We Use Your Data

CodNesta Group processes your data strictly for legitimate business, technical, and educational purposes:

  • Service Delivery: Building custom digital products, executing software development contracts, and managing client accounts.
  • Talent Development & Cohorts: Evaluating candidates, managing developer mentorship programs, tracking training progress, and processing graduations.
  • Communication: Responding to inquiries, sending project updates, delivering technical documentation, and providing administrative notices.
  • Platform Optimization: Enhancing user experience, debugging applications, and maintaining system infrastructure security.
  • Ecosystem Growth: Matching vetted tech talent and developers with corporate partners or project opportunities where explicitly opted in.

4. Legal Basis for Data Processing

We process personal data under established lawful grounds, including:

  • Consent: Explicit approval provided when submitting forms, applying for programs, or subscribing to communications.
  • Contractual Necessity: Processing required to fulfill agreements, build software deliverables, or provide requested services.
  • Legitimate Business Interests: Operating secure systems, improving engineering programs, and protecting against unauthorized access.
  • Legal Compliance: Adhering to applicable regulatory standards, including Cameroon's Law No. 2024/017 governing the protection of personal data and digital privacy rights.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

CodNesta Group does not sell or rent your personal information. We share data only with trusted entities required to operate our tech ecosystem:

  • Infrastructure & Hosting Providers: Secure cloud infrastructure and server deployment platforms.
  • Developer & Collaboration Tools: Version control, deployment pipelines, and internal communication workflows required for cohort and client projects.
  • Analytics Providers: Services like Google Analytics used to evaluate platform performance and traffic metrics.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: When strictly required by law, court order, or governmental regulations.

6. Cookies & Digital Tracking

Our digital platforms use essential and performance cookies to ensure optimal functionality:

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for secure navigation, session authentication, and dynamic form submissions.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us measure platform usage, identify technical bottlenecks, and improve content delivery.

You can manage or disable cookie preferences directly through your browser settings. Note that disabling essential cookies may impact certain dynamic features on our site.

7. Data Security & Retention

7.1 Security Safeguards

We employ technical and organizational measures—including HTTPS encryption, restricted database permissions, secure hosting, and strict access controls—to guard against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

7.2 Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to complete client engagements, maintain developer alumni records, resolve disputes, and comply with legal requirements. Outdated or unnecessary data is securely deleted or anonymized.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction and applicable data protection laws, you hold the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data CodNesta Group holds about you.
  • Correction: Update or rectify incomplete or inaccurate personal records.
  • Erasure: Request the deletion of your personal information where no longer required for legitimate contract execution or legal compliance.
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from promotional communications or developer community announcements at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, submit a written request to privacy@codnesta.com.
